# Skibidi Pizza

A responsive, Dutch-first pizza ordering experience for Delft, built with semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Switch between Dutch and English, and choose a light, dark, or kawaii theme; preferences are saved in the browser. Open `index.html` directly or serve the repository root with any static web server.

## Ordering flow

- Browse the menu by category and customize pizza size, crust, and extra toppings.
- Add pizzas to the bag, change quantities, remove items, and choose delivery or pickup.
- Review estimated tax and delivery fees, then submit the demo checkout form for a clear order-details confirmation.
- The cart is saved in the browser's local storage. Checkout does not process payments or send orders to a restaurant.
- Find the listed location at Brasserskade 1, Delft. Phone and email details are shown as coming soon because none were provided.

Prices are illustrative. Delivery is free from €35, otherwise €3.99; estimated tax is 8%. Menu pricing and the location are demo content, not a live restaurant or ordering service.
